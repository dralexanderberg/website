Alexander Berg

M.A., Ph.D. · Philosopher

I work on German Idealism – above all Hegel – and on Wittgenstein, and on the literary form of philosophy: how philosophical works are written, and why their form matters for what they say.

Until recently, I was a Feodor Lynen Research Fellow of the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation at the University of Zurich, where I am completing my habilitation.

Conference Series “Wittgenstein & Hegel”, 2017–2024

“Hegel seems to me to be always wanting to say that things which look different are really the same. Whereas my interest is in showing that things which look the same are really different.” Starting from this remark of Wittgenstein’s, I initiated a series of six international conferences – Dresden, Prague, Warsaw, Barcelona, Vienna and Zagreb – which I organized together with colleagues from across Europe. Three volumes have come out of it.

The documentation is kept here: programmes, abstracts, lists of talks, recordings and the resulting volumes.
