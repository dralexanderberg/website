Alexander Berg

Dr. phil. · Philosopher

I am a philosopher working in two fields. The first, Wittgenstein and Hegel, belongs to philosophy proper: I have written a monograph on it, edited two volumes and a journal special issue, and from 2017 to 2024 initiated and organized, together with colleagues, an international conference series. The second, which I have pursued at the University of Zurich since 2020, is the literary form of philosophy: from the methods of literary studies I develop a method for reading philosophical texts.

Until recently, I was a Feodor Lynen Research Fellow of the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation at the Universities of Zurich and Konstanz. I am completing my habilitation at the University of Zurich. It brings both fields together by testing this method on the main works of Hegel and Wittgenstein, the Phenomenology of Spirit and the Philosophical Investigations.
